The Writer

Gray is a Southern writer. Southern goth, softer around the edges. She writes with lyrical confessional precision — run-on sentences and Oxford commas with reckless abandon — and the result is work that sits at the edge of memoir and invention without apologizing for either.

She also makes music. Turtle Meat Mixtapes is where that lives.

The Character

Grace Lynn runs on intuition and consecrates everything she touches — commerce as ministry, chaos as liturgy. On the Porch she's the one who tied the pink bow on the cooler. She didn't ask anyone if that was appropriate. It wasn't a question.

The Porch version of Grace is the resolution of a longer story. You can feel the distance she's traveled in how lightly she moves now.
