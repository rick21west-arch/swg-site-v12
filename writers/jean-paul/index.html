Guild Mascot · Witness · Omniscient

Jean-Paul

Strangely sentient stuffed peacock. One eye cracked, one glowing blue-green since the Pensacola Incident. Already knows what's in the cooler.

Who He Is

Jean-Paul is not a joke character. This matters. He does serious narrative work as the only omniscient voice in the Porch world — witness to everything, participant in nothing, reporter of whatever the humans are too close to see clearly.

He was found. He has a pink bow Grace tied on him. He answers to French curses and banana pudding. One eye is cracked. The other has been glowing blue-green since the Pensacola Incident, details of which remain undisclosed.

"I emit low-level observational signal. The humans continue regardless."

The Cooler

Jean-Paul is inseparable from the cooler — found near a Waffle House dumpster, duct-taped, labeled MISC. BIOMEDICAL. Grace tied a pink bow on it. Nobody says what's in it. Jean-Paul already knows. The humans continue regardless.

On the Porch

Jean-Paul appears in every Porch story. He doesn't have to say much. He doesn't have to say anything. He was already there when you arrived.
