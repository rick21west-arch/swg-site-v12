The Character

Hank Cotton

Hotshot driver. Gritty realist. Hard-boiled truth delivered in plain English.

The Writer

MJ Polk

I'm Hank's handler. Not exactly sure why he trusts me with his stories, but he does. And I've agreed to pass them on to all of you. While Hank tramps around the USA playing Hotshot Hero — writing his memoir in shady diners and truck stops — I'm here in Southern Georgia, studying Philosophy and working on my craft.

The Character

Beau Pritchett IV

Regional nuisance. Minor legend. Ersatz poet who seized a guitar by the neck when he was a kid.

The Writer

Rick West

Rick West — the public-facing version, anyway — comes from the southeastern United States. The humid stretch of land where stories sweat before they speak. He wanders now. Portugal, Istanbul. Albania. Even Florida. Other coordinates still blinking on the map. He takes notes, asks bad questions, drinks whatever's poured, and passes the signal along. Author of First Light.

The Writer

Gray

My stories are sometimes fiction, always true. I'm not the Gray or Grace in these stories. But we've walked a lot of the same roads. Heard a lot of the same songs. Cried over a lot of the same things. For a long time, I lived mostly in my head. These days, I write it out. Author of the Opera Rose trilogy.
