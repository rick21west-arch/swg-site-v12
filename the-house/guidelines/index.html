How to Submit

We read everything.
We publish selectively.

Those two sentences can coexist.

The Southern Writers Guild exists to cultivate work with presence, texture, and a point of view that can stand on its own feet without shouting. We welcome submissions across forms and styles, but we curate with intention. Ruthlessly, when needed. Lovingly, when earned.

What we're looking for

We are interested in excellent work, full stop. That includes — but is not limited to:

  • Essays — personal, cultural, reflective, lyrical
  • Fiction — short stories, excerpts, strange hybrids
  • Poetry — tight, loose, or feral, but deliberate
  • Criticism and commentary — thoughtful, grounded, alive
  • Work that resists easy categorization but knows what it's doing

We're less interested in trends, clout, or hot takes. We're more interested in voice, coherence, and attention. If your piece feels like it had to be written, we want to see it.

What we are not looking for

Let's save each other some time.

  • Agenda-driven work. We have no truck with manifestos, party lines, or recruitment efforts of any kind.
  • Divisive writing. Controversy is welcome. Division is not. If the piece relies on flattening, scapegoating, or antagonizing entire groups, it's a no.
  • Performative outrage or moral posturing. If the work's primary fuel is indignation, it probably won't survive our editorial table.
  • Work that hasn't been finished thinking. Drafts are fine. Half-formed arguments masquerading as certainty are not.

This isn't about politeness. It's about depth.

Our editorial process

All submissions are reviewed by the Editorial Board. At present, the board consists of the three founding members of the Southern Writers Guild. Over time, this board may grow or change as the Guild evolves.

Decisions are made collaboratively, with an eye toward:

  • Craft
  • Voice
  • Resonance with the Guild's ethos
  • How the piece fits into the larger body of work we're curating

We do not publish by quota, trend, or pressure. We publish what holds up.

A note on curation

We curate carefully because we intend to protect the integrity of the space. This is not a stream. It's a place.

Every piece we publish shapes the tone, temperature, and trust of the Guild. That means acceptance rates may be low — not because we enjoy saying no, but because saying yes carries weight here.

Rejection is not a referendum on your worth. It's a reflection of fit, timing, and cohesion.

How to submit

  • Submit one piece at a time unless otherwise noted.
  • Include a brief cover note if you like. Keep it human.
  • Previously published work should be clearly labeled.
  • Simultaneous submissions are fine — just let us know if it's accepted elsewhere.

The Southern Writers Guild is for writers — and readers — who value attention over acceleration, clarity over noise, and work that can sit quietly in a room and still be felt.
