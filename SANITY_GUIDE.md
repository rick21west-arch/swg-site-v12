# Southern Writers Guild — Sanity Editing Guide

Where to go: **swg-studio.sanity.studio**. Sign in, click **Porch Story** on the left to see every story, or click **Create** to start a new one.

## Starting a new story

Click **Create → Porch Story**. Fill in, in order:

- **Title** — the story's name.
- **Slug** — click "Generate" and it builds this from the title automatically. This is the story's actual web address, so leave it alone once generated unless you have a specific reason to change it.
- **Published Date**
- **Authors** — e.g. "Hank Cotton," "Gray," "Beau," or a combination like "Beau + Gray." Don't use "all three" anymore — always name the actual writers.
- **Excerpt** — one or two sentences. Shows up on the card wherever this story appears in a grid.
- **Thumbnail** — upload an image here, or paste a URL if the image already lives somewhere else.

## Writing the story

The **Story Text** field is where the actual story goes. The toolbar across the top handles the basics: headings, bold, italic, bullet and numbered lists, and quotes. Beyond that:

**Inserting a link.** Highlight the words you want linked, click the link icon, and paste in a **complete web address** — `https://www.southernwritersguild.com/...` — never just a page name by itself. If you're linking to another Porch story, open that story first, copy what's in its **Slug** field, then build the address like this:

`https://www.southernwritersguild.com/the-porch/` + the slug + `/`

So linking to a story with the slug `cadi-mae` means pasting `https://www.southernwritersguild.com/the-porch/cadi-mae/` — not just `cadi-mae` by itself. Pasting just the slug is the single most common mistake here — it looks like it should work, but it sends readers to a broken page instead of the story.

**Inserting a divider.** Click the insert menu (the "+" in the toolbar) and choose **Divider**. Drops in a plain line to break the story into sections. No settings to fill in.

**Inserting an image.** Insert menu → **Image**. Upload the photo, add a caption if you want one, and choose a size: **Small**, **Medium**, **Large**, or **Full width**. Full width is the default and matches how images looked before this option existed — only change it if a particular photo looks better narrower.

**Inserting a video.** Insert menu → **Video**. Paste the full YouTube or Vimeo link. It'll show up as a real, playable video on the live page, not just a link.

**Inserting audio.** Insert menu → **Audio**. Paste a Spotify or SoundCloud link and it becomes a real embedded player. Any other kind of audio link (a Substack podcast page, for instance) still works, but shows up as a plain "Listen →" link instead of a player.

## The toggles at the bottom of a story

- **Show in main Porch grid** — on by default. Turn off to archive a story without deleting it.
- **Show on The Work — The Kitchen Table** — turn on for the more serious, essay-style pieces you want surfaced on The Work page.
- **Show on homepage** — turn on to feature a story in the "Right Now" section on the homepage. Only the two most recently turned-on stories show there at once, so turning a third one on bumps the oldest.
- **Substack URL** — only needed if a story still lives on Substack instead of here. Leave blank once the Story Text field above is filled in.

## Publishing

Nothing you do is visible to readers until you click **Publish** in the bottom right. Saving a draft (which happens automatically as you type) is not the same as publishing it.

## The two things worth double-checking before you publish

1. **Does the Slug field have something in it?** If it's empty, the story doesn't have a live web address yet, and nothing can link to it.
2. **If you linked to another story, is the address complete?** It should start with `https://www.southernwritersguild.com/` — if it's just a short word or two, it's wrong.
